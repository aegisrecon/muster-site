Muster

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Muster is a unit information app. It is operated by Aegis Recon ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

Who can use Muster

You cannot sign yourself up. A leader in your unit adds you by entering your e-mail address. If you did not expect to be added, contact your unit or us.

What we collect

  • Your e-mail address. Your unit leader enters it. We use it to send you a sign-in code and to identify your account.
  • Your name and rank, if your unit leader entered them.
  • Your unit and your role in it.
  • Your devices. A name for each phone you sign in on (for example "iPhone") and a random identifier the app creates, so that you can see your devices and sign a lost phone out.
  • What you post, if you are a leader: updates and unit page text.
  • A record of administrative actions, such as who added or removed a member and when, kept for security and accountability.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health information, Social Security number, DoD ID number or home address. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

Your PIN

The PIN you choose stays on your phone. It is never sent to us and we cannot recover it. If you forget it, you sign in again with a new code.

Who can see your information

  • Leaders of your unit, and of the units above it, can see your name, rank, e-mail address and role.
  • Other members of your unit do not see the member list.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts the app for us and processes the data on our behalf, in the United States.
  • Sign-in e-mails pass through e-mail providers on the way to your mailbox.

Keeping it and deleting it

  • A unit leader can remove you from a unit at any time. When you are no longer in any unit, your access ends.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Settings, then Delete my account. This removes you from every unit and deletes your sign-in and your devices. Updates you posted as a leader stay with the unit.
  • Administrative records are kept for as long as your organization uses Muster.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by role, and the app locks with your PIN. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

Content

Muster is for unclassified information that is not Controlled Unclassified Information. Do not post anything else.

Children

Muster is for adult members of the units that use it and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
