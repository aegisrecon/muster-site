Contact

E-mail support@aegisrecon.ai. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what you were doing.

I can't sign in

You cannot sign yourself up. A leader in your unit adds you by e-mail address. The first time, choose Activate my account and enter the 8-character access code from your invitation. After that, choose Sign in and enter the 6-digit code we e-mail you. Codes expire, so ask for a new one if yours no longer works.

I didn't get a code

Check that you entered the address your unit has for you, and look in your junk folder. If nothing arrives, ask your unit leader to confirm the address or resend your invitation.

I forgot my PIN

On the PIN screen tap Forgot PIN?. This signs the phone out. Sign in again with a new code and choose a new PIN. Your PIN stays on your phone and cannot be recovered by anyone.

I lost my phone

Sign in on another iPhone, open Settings from the gear on Home, and sign the lost phone out under My devices. A unit leader can also do this for you.

Delete my account

Open Settings from the gear on Home and choose Delete my account. This removes you from every unit and deletes your sign-in and devices. If you are the only owner of a unit, hand it over first.
